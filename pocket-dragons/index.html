    FAQ

    Good to know

    Is this the official Pocket Dragons website?

    No, it's a community hub. The official site is pocketdragons.io and official news comes from @Pocket_Dragons_ on X.

    Why do I sign in with email?

    No passwords: every time you sign in we email you a one-time 6-digit code. The first code creates your Keeper account. Your email is never shown to other Keepers.

    What can I share in Content?

    Links to your own public posts on X about Pocket Dragons: art, memes, guides, lore, videos. Posts are embedded straight from X. Spam and reposted art without credit get removed.

    Does the Hoard count towards badges, Honor or whitelist?

    No. The Hoard only reflects activity on this site. Badges, Honor and any whitelist rules are managed by the team on the official site.

    Will anyone here ask for my wallet or seed phrase?

    Never. Don't share your seed phrase, don't sign transactions from links posted in chat and be careful with DMs from "support".
